# knowledge expert cards


Scope of the 25 `knowledge` expert specifications. Each card is a capability specification, not a trained or validated expert.


---

## gk_ai_ml

Answer ai ml questions with checkable references, explicit uncertainty and dated sources for changing facts.

- Domain: knowledge; languages: hi, hinglish, en.
- Incoming: `Datasets/workbench/incoming/knowledge/gk_ai_ml.jsonl`.
- Taxonomy target rank: 128; small-model adapter parameters: 4,849,664.
- Initial pilot rank: 8; increase only after held-out improvement and memory profiling.
- Minimum independent examples: 1,000 train / 100 validation / 100 test.
- Follow the [shared dataset contract](../DATASET_SPEC.md), including provenance, family grouping, review and quality gates.
- A card is a capability specification, not a trained or validated expert.
