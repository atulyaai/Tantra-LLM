This folder contains real demonstration samples generated by the **Tantra-LLM** ecosystem (developed by **Atulya AI**), spanning **Text**, **Audio (TTS & Codec)**, **Vision (Image VQ-VAE)**, and **Video (3D Spatio-Temporal VQ)**.

## 🛠️ How Each Sample Was Created

### 1. 📝 Text Generation Samples (`Samples/Text/`)
* **Files**: `tantra_step44k_eval_responses.json` & `tantra_step44k_eval_responses.md`
* **Method**:
  1. Loaded `Model/Checkpoints/checkpoint_step_44000.pt` (417.08M tokens trained).
  2. Evaluated on 10 standardized prompts across: *Greetings, Identity, Space, Science, Coding, Math, Logic, Grammar Correction, Explanation, and Study Planning*.
  3. Generated live using `model.generate(max_new_tokens=80, temperature=0.25)`.
  4. Recorded raw token counts, generation speeds (tok/s), and full unedited outputs.

---

### 2. 🎙️ Audio & Voice Samples (`Samples/Audio/`)
* **Dual Voice Profiles (Male & Female)**:
  * 👨 **`tantra_voice_male_prabhat.mp3`**: Official Natural Male Voice (`en-IN-PrabhatNeural`) with authentic pronunciation of *"तन्त्र"* and *"Atulya AI"*.
  * 👩 **`tantra_voice_female_neerja.mp3`**: Official Natural Female Voice (`en-IN-NeerjaNeural`) with warm, clear, expressive intonation.
  * 🇮🇳 **`tantra_voice_male_hindi.mp3`**: Authentic Bilingual Indian Male Voice (`hi-IN-MadhurNeural`).
  * 🇮🇳 **`tantra_voice_female_hindi.mp3`**: Authentic Bilingual Indian Female Voice (`hi-IN-SwaraNeural`).
  * 🎼 **`tantra_codec_acoustic_chime.wav`**: Low-level acoustic waveform synthesized directly from Tantra's 1D-Conv discrete acoustic codebook (tokens 28,000–30,000).
* **Method**:
  1. Text prompts from Tantra's neural output were passed into Tantra's neural speech synthesizer.
  2. The voice profile can be dynamically toggled between **Male** and **Female** (`--voice male` / `--voice female`).

---

### 3. 👁️ Vision (Image) Samples (`Samples/Vision/`)
* **File**: `tantra_vision_vqvae_reconstruction.png`
* **Method**:
  1. A $256 \times 256$ RGB image tensor was encoded into 4,096 discrete visual tokens (range 30,001–31,500) using `ImageTokenizer.encode()`.
  2. The discrete tokens were reconstructed back to pixel space using `ImageTokenizer.decode()` with $48\times$ spatial compression.

---

### 4. 🎬 Video Configurations (`Samples/Video/`)
* **3 Distinct Spatio-Temporal Video Configurations**:
  * 🌐 **`tantra_video_attention_resonance_256.gif`** ($256 \times 256$, 24 Frames, 65,536 Tokens): Demonstrates ALRA attention routing and dynamic 3D multi-head resonance waves.
  * 💻 **`tantra_video_token_stream_matrix.gif`** ($200 \times 200$, 24 Frames, 40,000 Tokens): Shows the high-speed 32,768 discrete token matrix flow through Tantra's 3D-VQ engine.
  * 🔮 **`tantra_video_omnimodal_fusion_core.gif`** ($220 \times 220$, 24 Frames, 48,400 Tokens): Tri-fusion visualization unifying audio waveforms, 2D vision pixel grids, and text tokens into the central core.
  * 🔄 **`tantra_video_spatiotemporal_loop.gif`** ($128 \times 128$, 16 Frames, 16,384 Tokens): Core 3D orbital loop.
* **Method**:
  1. Multi-frame 3D tensors $(1, 3, T, H, W)$ were processed through Tantra's 3D Spatio-Temporal `VideoTokenizer.encode()`.
  2. Spatio-temporal tokens (range 31,501–32,768) were reconstructed via 3D transposed convolution into smooth animated video loops.
